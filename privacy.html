Privacy Policy

Snowballers, by App Studio 427 · Effective November 7, 2026

Snowballers home support@appstudio427.com

The short version

Snowballers doesn't collect personal information. There's no sign-in, no account, no chat, no analytics and no location tracking, and the game works completely offline. The App Store and Microsoft Store versions contain no advertising at all.

What is stored on your device

Two things, both kept only on your device and never sent to us:

  • Your progress: levels cleared, best scores, arenas unlocked, and your music, sound and control settings.
  • Your purchases: a record of which optional items you own, so the game works offline.

Uninstalling the game removes both. Purchases can be brought back with Restore Purchases in the game's Store.

Advertising

App Store (iPhone, iPad and Mac): no ads and no advertising software.

Microsoft Store (Windows and Xbox): no ads and no advertising software.

Google Play: the game shows one full-screen ad between levels, supplied by Google AdMob, never during play and no more than one every five minutes. Every ad request is tagged as child-directed and as coming from a user under the age of consent, and is limited to content rated G. According to Google's documentation, this means:

  • Personalised advertising and remarketing are turned off.
  • Requests to third-party ad vendors and measurement pixels are turned off.
  • The advertising identifier is not sent.

The optional Remove Ads subscription on Google Play stops all ad requests.

If a future version adds advertising to another store's version, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Purchases

Payment is handled entirely by the App Store, Google Play or the Microsoft Store. We never see or store your card, billing address or store account. When your device is online, the game checks with the store whether your purchases are still valid. When it's offline, it uses its last answer, so being offline never costs you something you paid for.

Children

Snowballers is made for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. There's no chat, no social feature and no way for a player to contact anyone through the game.

Your rights and deletion

We hold no personal information about you, so there's nothing for us to show you or delete. To remove everything the game has stored, uninstall it. Questions about a purchase go to the store that processed it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@appstudio427.com. Questions from parents and guardians are answered first.
