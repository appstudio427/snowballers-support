Roll one snowball down 1,000 levels, grow it big enough to bowl over the snow monster, then settle the score in Snowball Wars across 100 arenas. No internet needed.

Two games about the same snowball

Downhill

You're inside the snowball. Steer through the trees, pack on snow, and reach the bottom big enough to knock the snow monster flat. Too small, and he sends you back up the hill.

Snowball Wars

A throwing duel across 100 arenas. He ducks, he hides behind the igloo, and he throws two and three at once. Learn his wind-up and answer it, then face the snowman, the aliens and their ship.

Common questions

How do I get my purchases back?

Purchases belong to your store account. After reinstalling the game or moving to a new device, open the game, go to Store and tap Restore Purchases. You won't be charged again.

Does it need the internet?

No. Snowballers plays completely offline, and your progress is saved on your device.

What can I buy?

The whole game is free to play. Two optional one-time purchases are available: Unlock All Snowball Wars Arenas, which opens all 100 arenas straight away, and Ship & Photon Torpedo, which adds a seventh weapon. On Google Play there's also an optional Remove Ads subscription.

Are there ads?

Not on the App Store or the Microsoft Store. The Google Play version shows a short ad between levels, never during play, and every ad is set to child-safe content with personalised advertising turned off. See the privacy policy.

What controls can I use?

On-screen touch buttons, a keyboard, or a game controller. You can change the steering speed under Controls, and move the on-screen buttons to the other side of the screen.

Is it safe for kids?

Yes. There's no sign-in, no chat, no way to talk to other players, and the game collects no personal information. Purchases go through your store's own checkout, so parental controls on the device apply.
